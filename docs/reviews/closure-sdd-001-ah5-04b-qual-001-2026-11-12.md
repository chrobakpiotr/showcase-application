  Harness.

  ([runbook](../runbooks/unresolved-lifecycle-history.md)). The markers make the lifecycle refuse any mutation,
  which is what a closed feature needs, so no removal, recovery or state rewrite is performed.
- `spec.md`, `plan.md`, `tasks.json` and `verification-contract.json` are unchanged: they are fingerprinted and
  digest-bound.
- The October limitation record ([2026-10-25](showcase-recovery-state-limitations-2026-10-25.md)) remains the
  last observation of their state.

Reopening either feature as it stands is not a valid path; new work needs a new specification.
