Date 2026-11-11. CI failed for `fd052b0`, `10b80f3` and `28abf17`, and Agentic SDD CI failed for `10b80f3` and
`28abf17`. Job results and logs were read through the public GitHub Actions API.

## Fixes

- Spotless pins Prettier 3.8.1 with `singleQuote` and `trailingComma: es5`, the options that match the existing
  TypeScript style. All 26 affected frontend files were reformatted with Prettier 3.8.1 and these options.
  Most changes indent Angular control-flow blocks; three TypeScript files change by one line each.
- The test SKU prefix is `RC-`, 39 characters in total.
- The Playwright install step retries three times with 30 and 60 second pauses, like the existing image pull step.
- The trust anchor records the new hashes of `critical-postgres-manifest.json` and `spotless-prettier.gradle`.
  `test_verification_supervisor.py` pins the two new aggregate digests:
  `2935843bd8c2dea9f919c19b395862b8182823f9c35208d56c76ef2b29a93bdd` for the PostgreSQL control and
  `c2eeeb49416929f6f247f6761e987797479397cef6f45dc8896e5a7939789a82` for the RabbitMQ control.
